Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This page explains how Hudna handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Information you provide

You can use the app as a guest. If you create an account, we process the details needed to sign you in and keep your saved history and favorites. Entries you choose to record may describe feelings, eating decisions, or other personal experiences. Please avoid entering information you do not want to store.

Location data and Place Guard

When you use location features, Hudna may access your device’s approximate or precise location, depending on the permission you grant in Android settings. When you select “My location” on the map, the app uses your location to show where you are. You can choose a place and a notification radius around it.

If you enable Place Guard proximity alerts and grant the required permissions, Hudna uses precise location in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use, to notify you when you enter the area around a chosen place. Receiving alerts may also require app notifications and device location services to be enabled.

The app stores your selected place information on your device, including coordinates, notification radius and place settings, and uses Google Play location services to register proximity areas. Google Maps and Google Places process service requests when you use the map or search for a place. Search requests include the text you enter and may include the boundaries of the map area displayed to improve results. These services may also process technical information such as IP address, device and service version information, service-specific identifiers, crash information and map interactions to operate and improve Google services.

You can turn off place alerts in the app, edit or delete places, and withdraw or change the accuracy of location permission in your device settings; location-dependent features may stop working. For questions or deletion requests, contact fadyf260@gmail.com or see our account deletion instructions.

Places saved on the device remain available for the feature unless you delete them or the app data is removed. The app may retain local owner-bound copies to protect data when accounts change; deleting a place does not necessarily erase every saved copy immediately. When an account is deleted, the app attempts to remove active local data and copies confirmed to belong to that account, while preserving data whose ownership has not been established. Contact us about deletion requests.

Google processes map request data under the Google Privacy Policy and acts as an independent controller for Google Maps Platform data under its terms. According to Google Support for Places SDK 4.4.1, Google may retain technical logs including request metadata and IP addresses for security and service reliability. Support states that users cannot directly request deletion of these logs through standard Google Account tools. Retention varies by data type and purpose; see Google’s retention policy.

App usage analytics

Some Android app versions include Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app usage and improve the app. Depending on service settings and operation, Google may process information such as app opens, session duration, app and operating system versions, device information, approximate region and an app-installation identifier. The versions we reviewed disable advertising ID collection; this does not mean other identifiers are not used. Google processes this information under its privacy policy and the retention settings that apply to the analytics service. The app-linked analytics property is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months, with user data retention reset on new activity. Aggregated reports and other Google data may be governed by different retention rules.

Depending on how you use Hudna, information may include an email address and user ID; emotions, urge intensity and eating context you enter or select; exercise sessions, interventions, outcomes and challenges; favorites and AI feedback; and technical diagnostics. The information processed varies with the features and services you use, as described in this policy.

Subscriptions and purchase history

When you purchase or restore a Hudna subscription through Google Play, we process purchase information to verify your subscription, enable Plus features, restore access, and prevent the same purchase from being used across different Hudna accounts. The app may automatically recheck purchases when you sign in. This information includes the purchase token needed for verification, your Hudna account identifier, product and plan identifiers, order identifier, subscription and renewal status, start and expiry dates, and purchase country where supplied by Google Play.

Hudna's backend processes the purchase token to verify it with Google Play. Subscription records retain a one-way fingerprint of the token and subscription status information linked to your account. Google Cloud Run and Supabase support this processing and storage. Google Play handles payment; Hudna does not request or receive your card number or card security code through Google Play purchases.

The Google Play integration for purchase reporting in Firebase and Google Analytics is not currently enabled, and the app does not send custom purchase events to Analytics. Hudna uses purchase information to verify and restore subscriptions and prevent misuse. General usage analytics remain enabled in versions that include Firebase. Under the current data-sharing setting, Google may use analytics data to improve its products and services under its terms. Disabling the advertising identifier does not disable all analytics identifiers.

Subscription-verification data is sent to Hudna's backend over HTTPS, and the backend communicates with Google Play over HTTPS. Google documents TLS encryption in transit for Analytics data. Subscription processing is not solely ephemeral: status records are stored to support access and restoration.

Purchase-state, entitlement, payment and subscription-audit records are linked to your account. The current scheduled retention process does not delete these records simply because a subscription expires. Your linked records are deleted from the active database when deletion of your authentication account completes successfully. This does not mean immediate deletion of infrastructure backups or Google's independent records.

You can manage or cancel subscription renewal in Google Play and request deletion of your Hudna account data in the app or through our account-deletion page. Canceling renewal and deleting a Hudna account are separate actions; do not assume that deleting the account cancels the Google Play subscription. Google may retain its transaction and analytics records under its own policies.

How information is used

We use account and app data to provide the features you choose, keep your information available across sessions, support your account, verify and restore subscriptions, prevent purchase misuse, and improve reliability. Hudna does not use your personal app entries for advertising.

AI features

Where an optional AI feature is offered, relevant text may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Do not enter information you do not wish to share with that provider. AI responses are informational and can be inaccurate.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase supports authentication and account data storage; Google Cloud Run hosts the backend API; Gemini API processes AI requests; Google Sign-In supports optional Google login; and Google Play distributes the app and handles payments and subscription verification. Google Analytics for Firebase measures app usage; its current account setting also permits Google to use analytics data to improve Google products and services. We do not sell user data or share app entries with independent advertisers. Supabase and Cloud Run process information to operate your account and services. Google Analytics data sharing for Google products and services, and Google Maps Platform processing, have the purposes and terms described above; these uses are not all processing solely on Hudna's behalf.

Website analytics and marketing

The website may use Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and TikTok Pixel if configured. These tools are loaded only after the relevant consent. You can change your choice through “Tracking settings” on the site. Marketing tools and GTM run only on the homepages. GA4 can run on public pages after analytics consent. Providers may receive ordinary visit data such as page title, browser or device information, and IP address under their own policies. App account details and health answers are not sent from this site to these tools. See the consent panel for the currently enabled categories.

Storage, security, and retention

We use service providers to host the website and app data, and take reasonable measures to protect information. No internet service can promise absolute security. Account data is kept while your account is active and removed after a valid deletion request, subject to limited legal or security retention. Technical logs and backups may remain for a limited period before being deleted under provider schedules.

Current retention periods

Scheduled deletion targets the periods below; deletion can take longer if a job fails or data is backlogged. Local records whose age cannot be determined are retained to protect user data.

  • Exercise sessions: target 365 days.
  • Emotional eating logs, urge events, intervention events, outcomes, challenge events, and AI feedback: target 180 days.
  • Daily AI usage records: target 7 days.
  • Purchase-state, entitlement, payment and subscription-audit records are linked to the account. The current scheduled retention process does not delete them simply because a subscription expires; linked records are removed from the active database when authentication-account deletion completes successfully.
  • Local wellbeing history on the device: a 180-day window is applied when dated records are read; records whose age cannot be determined may be retained.
  • Cloud Run logs routed to the default Cloud Logging bucket: 30 days; retention audit logs: target 90 days.

Raw AI prompts and responses are not ordinarily kept as a permanent app history. When an authenticated account deletion completes, the authentication account and app records covered by database deletion relationships are removed, including exercise and favorites records. Analytics data and service-provider logs may follow their own retention and deletion schedules. Some backups or technical logs may remain under their applicable retention periods, and local data with unverified ownership may be retained to protect its owner. Guest history stored locally can be deleted in the app or by clearing app data; some server or AI features may still send necessary context for real-time processing.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or delete information associated with your account. You may also withdraw optional website tracking consent at any time. To delete your account, use the in-app option or follow our account deletion instructions.

Contact

For privacy questions, email fadyf260@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the service changes; the date above shows the latest revision.
