Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. This page explains how Hudna handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Information you provide

You can use the app as a guest. If you create an account, we process the details needed to sign you in and keep your saved history and favorites. Entries you choose to record may describe feelings, eating decisions, or other personal experiences. Please avoid entering information you do not want to store.

Depending on how you use Hudna, information may include an email address and user ID; emotions, urge intensity and eating context you enter or select; exercise sessions, interventions, outcomes and challenges; favorites and AI feedback; limited technical diagnostics. Under the current implementation we do not declare collection of location, financial information, messages, photos or videos, audio, files, calendar, contacts, browsing history, device or advertising IDs, or crash logs as app data categories.

How information is used

We use account and app data to provide the features you choose, keep your information available across sessions, support your account, and improve reliability. Hudna does not use your personal app entries for advertising.

AI features

Where an optional AI feature is offered, relevant text may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Do not enter information you do not wish to share with that provider. AI responses are informational and can be inaccurate.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase supports authentication and account data storage; Google Cloud Run hosts the backend API; Gemini API processes AI requests; Google Sign-In supports optional Google login; and Google Play distributes the app. We do not sell user data or share app entries with independent advertisers. These providers process information to operate the features you use.

Website analytics and marketing

The website may use Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and TikTok Pixel if configured. These tools are loaded only after the relevant consent. You can change your choice through “Tracking settings” on the site. Marketing tools and GTM run only on the homepages. GA4 can run on public pages after analytics consent. Providers may receive ordinary visit data such as page title, browser or device information, and IP address under their own policies. App account details and health answers are not sent from this site to these tools. See the consent panel for the currently enabled categories.

Storage, security, and retention

We use service providers to host the website and app data, and take reasonable measures to protect information. No internet service can promise absolute security. Account data is kept while your account is active and removed after a valid deletion request, subject to limited legal or security retention. Technical logs and backups may remain for a limited period before being deleted under provider schedules.

Current retention periods

  • Exercise sessions: up to 365 days.
  • Emotional eating logs, urge events, intervention events, outcomes, challenge events, and AI feedback: up to 180 days.
  • Daily AI usage records: up to 7 days.
  • Local wellbeing history on the device: up to 180 days.
  • Cloud Run logs: up to 30 days; retention audit logs: up to 90 days.

Raw AI prompts and responses are not ordinarily kept as a permanent app history. An authenticated account deletion removes associated data from active systems when the deletion flow completes, independently of these routine periods. Guest history stored locally can be deleted in the app or by clearing app data; some server or AI features may still send necessary context for real-time processing.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or delete information associated with your account. You may also withdraw optional website tracking consent at any time. To delete your account, use the in-app option or follow our account deletion instructions.

Contact

For privacy questions, email fadyf260@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the service changes; the date above shows the latest revision.
