Delete your Hudna account

You can delete your Hudna account and its associated data directly in the app. This is the primary method and does not require an email request.

Delete from the app

Open the Hudna Android app and sign in. Tap “My Account” in the bottom bar. Under “Account and Privacy”, choose “Request account deletion”, or under the sensitive area choose “Delete my account”. Read the warning and confirm permanent deletion. The app verifies your signed-in session and removes associated data from active systems without waiting for manual email approval.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email fadyf260@gmail.com from the email linked to your account, with “Hudna account deletion” in the subject. We may verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens to your data

The authenticated deletion flow removes your account ID and email from authentication, exercise and emotional eating records, urge and intervention events, outcomes, challenges, favorites, activity data, and any associated AI feedback from active app systems. Temporary provider backups or infrastructure copies may expire on their own replacement schedules. Please see our privacy policy for more detail.

Guest and local data

Guest wellbeing history is stored on your device. Delete it in the app or by clearing the app’s data in device settings. Server or AI features may send context for real-time processing; raw prompts and responses are not ordinarily saved as a permanent app history.

Need help?

Contact fadyf260@gmail.com with any questions.
