About Hudna

Hudna means a pause: a small, quiet space between a feeling and a decision. The Arabic Android app helps you notice whether you may need food, rest, support, or a moment to breathe.

Our approach

There are no calorie targets, weight goals, or shame. Hudna offers short reflections and exercises, reminders for recurring times, support before ordering out, and a weekly reflection for registered users.

You can begin as a guest. Creating an account lets you save your history and favorites in the app.

Who it is for

Hudna is for people who want to better understand the connection between feelings and eating. It is a self-help tool, not a diet plan or medical treatment. If eating causes persistent distress, please speak with a qualified professional.
