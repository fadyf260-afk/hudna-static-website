What is emotional eating?

Eating can sometimes be a response to stress, boredom, sadness, or physical hunger. A calm pause can help you notice what you need without judging yourself. Explore the difference between physical and emotional hunger.

Is Hudna a diet?

No. The app does not count calories or ask for your weight. It helps you pause and reflect before eating.

Can I start without an account?

Yes, you can start as a guest. An account lets you save your history and favorites in the app.
